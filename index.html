Falk Hobrecht is a filmmaker and artist based in Berlin. His work combines documentary observation, research and moving image, with a particular interest in landscapes and places shaped by social and economic processes.

He studied documentary film at the Hochschule für bildende Künste Hamburg and at the École des Beaux-Arts de Marseille.
