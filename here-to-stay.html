Here to Stay 2026—
Documentary film, work in progress, 2026—

At the edge of Germany's Hambach open-pit mine, entire villages have disappeared, while new settlements take their place.

Moving between these settlements, the remaining forest, and the vast excavation, the film observes a landscape shaped by extraction, displacement, and the promise of a future beyond coal.
